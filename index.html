How to play

Find your position. Read the play. Pick your spot.

GET SETRunner on firstYou are playing pitcher3

1. Get set

The three-second card shows the runners and your position. Your player glows teal. The hit is a surprise—watch where the ball goes when the card disappears.

2. Tap your destination

Touch or click the actual A, B, or C circle on the field where your player should go. You can also use the matching answer buttons below or beside the field. Pick a destination—not the player’s starting spot.

Illustration only; each play has its own correct answer.

3. Watch, learn, repeat

All five infielders move to the correct spots after your answer. Read the explanation or replay the rotation. Then tap anywhere on the field for the next play. Finish practice—or finish the challenge—to review your missed plays.

Practice or challenge?

Practice: no time limit. Learn at your own pace.

Challenge: 90 seconds, +10 for a correct answer and −5 for a wrong answer. Scores can go below zero.

The clock pauses from your next-play tap through the three-second setup and hit animation. It resumes when the ball stops. It runs while you choose, watch the defensive rotation, or read explanations—including this help.
